Privacy Policy — YangShengWen Asset Studio

Last updated: 2026-09-26

This privacy policy describes the personal Pinterest integration used by YangShengWen Asset Studio. The application is operated by one owner for the owner's own Pinterest account and is not offered as a service to other users.

Information processed

When the owner connects Pinterest, the local application processes the OAuth access token and refresh token required to act on the owner's behalf. It may also process the owner's board names, Pin metadata, Pin identifiers and organic Pin analytics. If the optional Gumroad integration is enabled, the application may read the owner's product sales and revenue records.

How information is used

The information is used only to publish the owner's approved marketing Pins, match Pins to the owner's boards, synchronize performance metrics and maintain local project records. The application does not sell the information, use it for advertising to third parties or collect information from other Pinterest users.

Storage and retention

Credentials and synchronized records are stored locally on the owner's Windows computer in an ignored configuration file and local CSV/JSON logs. They are not included in digital-product ZIP files or public source control. The owner may delete the local credentials and records at any time.

Sharing

The application sends requests to Pinterest only as needed for the owner's requested integration. No personal information is shared with unrelated third parties.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner through the public YangShengWen-0505 GitHub profile.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be reflected on this public page.
