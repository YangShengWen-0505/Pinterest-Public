YangShengWen AI Asset Studio

AI-assisted digital asset creation and Pinterest marketing automation.

YangShengWen AI Asset Studio is a personal project for creating, organizing and promoting digital products. It prepares approved marketing assets and can publish the owner's approved Pins to the owner's Pinterest boards through the Pinterest API.

What this project provides

  • AI-assisted creation of marketing images and product assets.
  • Organization and analysis of digital-product promotion materials.
  • Personal Pinterest publishing and analytics workflows.
